## Incident Report
### Ransomware Attack on 2023-05-02

#### Summary
**Confidence:** Medium  
The attack started with failed login attempts from an external IP address (185.220.101.47), followed by successful logins and unusual activity, such as the opening of suspicious documents and the creation of a ransom note.

#### Root Cause
**Confidence:** Medium  
The root cause of the attack is likely related to the external IP address (185.220.101.47) accessing internal systems, but we cannot confirm if this was a direct or indirect compromise.

#### Runbook Steps
The following steps were completed:

The following steps were missed:

* Pre-stage tools (EDR/AV console, firewall access)
* Document critical assets, admin accounts, and network segmentation maps

#### Uncertainties & Flags

* **FLAG:** We cannot confirm the source of the external IP address (185.220.101.47).
* **FLAG:** The timeline of events is unclear due to missing timestamps in some logs.
* **FLAG:** The identity of patient zero and initial access vector are unknown.

#### Recommended Next Actions
1. Investigate the source of the external IP address (185.220.101.47) and its connection to internal systems.
2. Review network traffic logs for suspicious activity and investigate any potential C2 beaconing.
3. Perform a thorough analysis of endpoint events, including process executions and file modifications.
4. Rebuild from golden images for affected hosts and restore systems from known-good backups.
5. Rotate at-risk credentials and review privileged access to prevent future reinfections or repeated attacks.
